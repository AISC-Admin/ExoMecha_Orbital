Satellite deployment -- a 3-stage roadmap

A 3D visualization of the TITAN ORBITAL orbital constellation, rolled out progressively from Year 0 to Year 1.5, under review by Pr. Ambrosini, a satellite mission design specialist: exact positioning and a full technical data sheet for every satellite.

Deployment roadmap

From Option A to the target ring -- 12 to 28 satellites in 18 months

Each stage is weighed against the same requirement: a permanent, low-risk link between ExoMecha's proprietary datacenters and its regional agencies.

Year 0

Option A -- 12 satellites

A dedicated orbital plane (4 planes, i=55°, RAAN 40/130/220/310) for the Tallinn <-> West Africa corridor. Direct line of sight, no relay and no technology bet: validates the full technical chain before the target fleet. 95% availability, 13-minute maximum outage.

Year 1.5 (target)

Target ring -- 16 satellites

A shared ring (4 planes, i=60°) covering 4 zones simultaneously (West Africa, Europe, China-Hong Kong, Brazil) and closing the 3 priority corridors (Mali-Tallinn, Tallinn-Hong Kong, Dakar-Recife) to a 0-minute outage, subject to an inter-satellite laser range of at least 20,000 km (40,000 km confirmed).

Method

Keplerian simulation

Two-body propagation without J2, Wertz coverage geometry, 20° elevation threshold (Ka band) -- pre-study orders of magnitude, to be verified by a dedicated mission analysis (GMAT/STK).
