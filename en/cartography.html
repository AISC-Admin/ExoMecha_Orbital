Corporate mapping of the TITAN ORBITAL consortium

A 3D globe and flat map of the 22 legal entities in the consortium, their 21 capital and functional relationships, and a 5-year valuation module based on sector comparables.

What this tool shows

22 entities, 7 jurisdictions, 21 relationships

Each point on the globe is a legal entity in the consortium; each arc is a capital or functional relationship, weighted by a link-strength score from 1 to 5.

Entities

Detail card on hover

Capital, legal form, shareholders and officers, activity, country -- for each of the 22 entities, including Mali entities whose data is verified against source documents (trade register, tax IDs, business license, minutes of the extraordinary general meeting).

Relationships

Graded link strength

21 capital and functional relationships between entities; arc thickness and glow reflect link strength, from thematic/unconfirmed to very strong.

Valuation

Sector comparables

An adjustable 5-year valuation module (users, growth, ARPU) multiplied by neobank/fintech EV/Revenue multiples (low 2.6x, median 3.7x, high 10x).

Jurisdictions covered

A consortium spread across several continents

The mapped entities span multiple legal and tax jurisdictions, reflecting the group's operational, regulatory and banking-license footprint.

1

Estonia -- registered seat of the group (ExoMecha OU) and its R&D / blockchain subsidiaries (PROGENIS BioSystems, ExoLedger).

2

Delaware, United States -- six entities, each capitalized at USD 10,000, including A.I.S.C Space Exploration Technologies LLC and Intelligence Consortium corp LLC.

3

Mali -- A.I.S.C Space Exploration Technologies S.A (spaceport) and Intelligence Consortium corp SAS (logistics), both verified against source legal documents.

4

France, Cyprus and planned banking subsidiaries -- SkyN DeepTech, FUTURA GreenTech, BIONIC STRATOM Ltd, plus upcoming agencies in Hong Kong, Lithuania, El Salvador and Switzerland.
