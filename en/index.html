The project

A core banking system notarized by a proprietary constellation

TITAN ORBITAL is the architecture linking ExoMecha's core banking system -- including its blockchain function -- to a dedicated satellite constellation. The satellites have one clearly defined role: cryptographic notary/witness via threshold-signature key custody, periodic ledger checkpointing, a verifiable randomness source for consensus leader election, and the transport of daily banking traffic between the proprietary datacenters and regional agencies.

The full ledger, smart-contract execution and core banking state remain hosted in proprietary terrestrial datacenters (Lithuania or Estonia, Hong Kong, Mali) -- a regulatory traceability requirement (DORA) as much as an architectural choice.

  • Cryptographic notarization and ledger checkpointing via threshold signature
  • Daily banking traffic relay between proprietary datacenters and regional agencies
  • Ledger, smart contracts and banking state always hosted on the ground

ExoMecha & AISC Space

A banking architecture, an African industrial anchor

ExoMecha works alongside A.I.S.C Space Exploration Technologies SA, a Malian company progressively deploying a spaceport in Bamako and building an integrated African space sector -- launch, engineering, solar energy and research.

This partnership grounds TITAN ORBITAL's orbital ambition in an industrial reality: the same continent that hosts the Mali hub of the ExoMecha banking network is also, in time, home to part of the launch capacity its constellation will need.
